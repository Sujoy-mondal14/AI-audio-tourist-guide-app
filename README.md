🎧 AI Audio Tour Guide
Your Personal Historian in Your Pocket. The AI Audio Tour Guide is a context-aware, generative audio platform that transforms any location into an immersive storytelling experience. Using real-time GPS data and Large Language Models (LLMs), it creates personalized, conversational tours on the fly—no pre-recorded scripts required.

✨ Features
📍 Real-Time Contextual Awareness: Uses GPS to identify landmarks, architecture, and hidden gems around you.

🎙️ Generative Narration: AI-driven storytelling that adapts to your interests (e.g., "History Buff" mode vs. "Architecture Geek").

🗣️ Natural Voice Synthesis: High-quality, human-like neural voices for an effortless listening experience.

🌍 Multilingual Support: Instantly translate and narrate tours in over 30+ languages.

📸 Visual Recognition (Optional): Point your camera at a statue or building to get an instant deep-dive.
